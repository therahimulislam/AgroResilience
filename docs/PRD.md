# AgroResilience

Product Requirements Document (PRD)

Version: 1.0
Status: Hackathon MVP

1. Product Vision

AgroResilience helps farmers make more climate-aware decisions by turning farm, soil, weather and satellite signals into simple, localized actions.

2. Problem

Farmers often make decisions using established practices and previous-season experience. Those practices may become less reliable when rainfall, temperature, soil condition, water availability and crop stress change.

The product addresses the gap between complex agricultural data and an understandable farm-level decision.

3. Target User

Primary user: Indian farmer or farm operator using a smartphone or web browser.

Secondary users: agricultural educators, researchers, hackathon evaluators, NGOs and future institutional users.

4. Product Promise

Instead of exposing raw environmental datasets, AgroResilience answers three questions:

What is happening on my farm?

Why is it happening?

What should I consider doing next?

6. Product Metrics for the Demo

A complete farm can be created in under 3 minutes.

A farm analysis can be initiated from one primary CTA.

The dashboard communicates the primary risk in under 10 seconds of visual scanning.

At least one AI question can be answered with farm-specific context.

The complete demo path can be run from the deployed link without developer intervention.

7. Experience Principles

Evidence before advice

Simple before technical

Explainable before impressive

One clear action per section

Voice as an access path, not a separate product

Mobile-first interaction

8. Success Definition

The product succeeds for the hackathon when judges can see real inputs flowing through real or clearly identified development providers, into an intelligence layer, into a visible farm decision workflow.
